## 1. Principles

- **P1** The map is primary; geometry is its image. A device = atlas of thickened chart maps + interfaces + flat content; the device solid is the image of F̂ on the material region. Joint closures are export-layer solidization (§10), not device material.
- **P2** Exactness allocation: **chart geometry exact** · **authored flat content exact** · **material correspondence certified** — content-induced 3D geometry and transported flat objects are certified in anchored backward-error form: one anchor (point or spline) per item, one refinable stamp, exact fiber-fields over anchors, no compounding. Identities structural in the ring; inequalities certified with slacks.
- **P3** Flat is authoritative, always. 3D-side authoring is a projection gesture terminating in a **freeze** into exact flat content with provenance; derived marks are advisory, never fab geometry.
- **P4** Developability is a stratum, not an axiom. Load-bearing invariants: rationality of the map over the coefficient field; straight w-lines.
- **P5** All booleans in flat. Authored content is exact and closed under exact-predicate booleans; fab geometry is composed of authored operands only, the **freeze** being the sole certified→authored gate (generative pullbacks included). Derived marks participate only in three-valued tube predicates {holds, fails, undecided(ε)} with declared conservative direction. No kernel CSG.
- **P6** Every non-exact quantity carries a certificate: statement, class, decidability, bound, method, refinement recipe. Role and evaluation are orthogonal: "query-grade" is a role label (never authoritative, never a class-A atom), not an exemption — every numerical evaluation carries its class-T stamp.

## 2. Conventions

Units mm. σ = chart parameter, μ = ruling coordinate, w = transversal coordinate; ′ = d/dσ; n points toward w > 0; frames right-handed — noting that the *derived* tangent pair (r/ρ, n′/ρ) is **negatively** oriented for n (determinant −1 against n); the positively oriented tangent frame is (r/ρ, −n′/ρ), the one the development maps (§4.1).

### 2.2 Coefficient field lattice [D3 resolved; knobs D22]
- **L0 — declared-quantum rationals.** Per-source quanta in the manifest (10⁻⁶ mm KiCad; Gerber decimal steps; 25.4/10⁷ mm Altium; per-value 2^e for binary-float imports). Mixed-quantum predicates rescale **once to the gcd quantum — integers over the lcm of denominators — per predicate**; the common grid is a per-predicate ephemeral, never a global. No forced common grid; cross-quantum snapping per-input, ledgered; computed geometry rounds one-sidedly into the declared export grid at emission; native-quantum content round-trips exactly (GRID-closure).
- **L1 — K-towers.** Chart-grade and spec-authored constructions: K = ℚ(α₁,…,α_m), real-algebraic generators with defining polynomials and isolating intervals; canonical tower form; default budget height ≤ 3, degree ≤ 8 (the compass-and-straightedge diet; 42° is constructible and carried exactly). Datum unit-norm generators live here.
- **L2 — lazy √-DAGs** over L0; adaptive intervals; structural hashing; normalized into L1 within budget.
- **L3 — reserved**: real-algebraic numbers (minimal polynomial over L1, isolating interval), Sturm-comparable — needed only for constructed boolean vertices beyond degree 2. **v1 content curves = lines + circular arcs** [D24] (degree-≤2 intersections stay in-lattice); the same class bounds **constructed closure caps** (CAP-D24, §8.5); predicates never need L3.

Decision procedures: identities by node/vector sharing; strict inequalities by interval refinement (terminating on the open margins of §3.2); exact zero-tests confined to K-exact identity checks via separation bounds, terminal `unresolved(margin)`. **Predicate discipline:** squared distances (no √); computed conservative regions round one-sidedly (semantically exact for conservative roles). Angle snapping optional, per-input, ledgered — a cost choice, never the exactness mechanism.

### 2.3 Angles and rotations
Angles never appear in stored data or class-A certificates. Angular *predicates* use the half-angle/winding/pole calculus (§8.3). Rotational *families* are carried by **rational half-angle vectors** — T = [(1−|v|²)n + 2v]/(1+|v|²) for directors, q(s) = 1 + s·V for fan sweeps, the deg-1 quaternion line for tag arcs: three appearances of one idiom (tangential stereographic), one rule: *uniform physical angle never parametrizes anything.* Angular *evaluations* (θ(t), schedules) are class-T stamped enclosures.

**Gauge-transcription rule.** Arclength/analysis-gauge identities (|x′| = 1, |n′| = 1) are explanatory only; every stored field is written in the record's own rational parameter, and Jacobians are computed in the stored coordinates.

### 2.4 w-convention
w = 0 is the chart-local **stackup-geometric midplane** per stackup region ((w⁻+w⁺)/2 = 0; declared asymmetric exceptions [D19]); w-intervals are chart-local and measure **along-fiber (material) thickness**; assembly relations are relative support data, never absolute w-shifts (the shared-reference two-to-one view exists only as the `project_onto` query). w = 0 is bookkeeping; the strain and development reference is the z_N fiber.

### 2.6 Storage forms and continuity terms
Stored rationals (h, μ̂±, u, ε(s), content arcs, profiles) in **positive-weight NURBS form** (denominator ≥ min wᵢ structurally); general weights = certified opt-out (REG-RAT). q is polynomial by design. **Phase-blindness lemma:** strip semantics factor through (n, h); q's scale and phase fibers carry no semantic content on strips; gauge-fixing is recorded hygiene [D10a/b]. **MONO** = material fibers cross the joint locus; **BONDED** = the locus is a cut realized by assembly (blank count irrelevant — a dart is one blank, BONDED). v1 implements MONO; BONDED reserved (§14).

## 3. Chart

### 3.1 Kinds
`strip` (|n̂′| > 0 per span, after deflation where flagged) and `planar` (n′ ≡ 0 per span — a coefficient identity, never inferred from a point zero).

### 3.2 Strip

**Primal data** (q, h): q a polynomial quaternion spline, h a positive-weight rational spline [D2]; C¹ at interior knots. Derived, σ-rational over K:

```
n = q e₃ q̄/|q|²          exact unit normal (Euler–Rodrigues)
r = n × n′                ruling direction (gauge-scaled)
c = h n + (h′/|n′|²) n′   pedal point of the ruling (c·r = 0; line-invariant)
X = c + μ r               base surface
C = X + w n               thickened map — linear in (μ, w)
```

Offsets in-family: h ↦ h + w. Calibration per §2.5.

**Candidate vs valid.** (q, h, dom) is a candidate; **developability is an identity** (K = 0 wherever immersed; |n| = 1; offsets-in-family — unconditional); **validity is the ladder** REG ∧ SLAB ∧ CLEAR of strict inequalities. **Openness holds in stored-parameter space**: the data model *eliminates* structural equalities (handles, derived mates, plateaus, unit directors, face identities) — stored parameters are coordinates on the structural stratum; unconstrained coefficient space is reachable only through imports (§8.2). Certificate **slacks** are barrier values with exact-or-certified gradients (interior-point-consumable); a trust radius = slack over a certified sensitivity bound on a declared box; slacks are *not* coefficient-space distances; raw slacks never aggregate across kinds.

**Second-order identities:**

```
ψ′ = det(n, n′, n″)/|n′|²        (deflation-invariant)
det J = (c′ + μ r′)·n′ + w|n′|²  = |n′|²(R₁ + w),   1/κ₁ = det J/|n′|²  (exact size field)
```

*Degenerations of (n, h), kept distinct:* n′-concentration ⇒ dihedral **crease** (a Gauss-map jump across a shared ruling — an interface object, inexpressible inside one smooth chart); h″⁺-concentration ⇒ **planar facet** between parallel rulings (tangent planes continuous, no dihedral); R₁ → 0 ⇒ regression. Interior C¹ knots = curvature jumps = straight-G1 geometry.

#### 3.2.1 Isolated |n′| zeros — taxonomy and removability

**Parity first, always.** Split trigger: **sgn p differs across σ*** ⟺ k = ord(n′) odd (in the factoring case, equivalently the substitution has even local degree — χ = σ² has k = 1). Odd-k branches cover **opposite μ-sides of the same rulings** (X(−σ, μ) = X(σ, −μ)): mandatory split at σ* — the S-bend/inflection configuration, carried as two spans, never one.

**Removable ⟺ the complete record factors, with weights.** Geometric factoring n = N∘χ, h = H∘χ (deg χ ≥ 2; gauge handled by factoring rational n and re-lifting the Hopf section, legal by D10a) is necessary, not sufficient. **Canonical quotient chart — the substitution's output is a standard chart.** On each post-parity one-sided span let **ε = sgn χ′** (constant by SUBST-MONO) and canonicalize the quotient parameter: **s = ε·χ(σ)** (s′ = |χ′| > 0), **N̄(s) = N(εs), H̄(s) = H(εs)**. The standard strip derivation applied to (N̄, H̄) then yields the physical ruling and coordinate with **no map-level orientation bit**: N̄ × N̄_s = ε(N × N_χ) = R_ε, μ_std = ν_ε = |χ′|μ; the signed ℓ = ν_ε|N̄_s| is invariant on both branches; bound ordering is preserved; ψ̄′ = ε·ψ′∘χ (turning negates when the Gauss path reverses — automatic, no clause). The orientation packet is a **construction-time record**: consumed once at chart birth (argument flip, field transport, domain ordering, interface orientation), retained as provenance and in the shared χ-primitive tag (per-branch ε); after birth the chart is standard and packet-free.

**Field transport — two independent attributes** (value/basis law × argument signature), tensor-bundle style; the base map is one object, stated once:

```
(σ, μ)  ↦  (s, ν_ε)  =  (ε·χ(σ), |χ′(σ)|·μ)
```

| value/basis law | argument signature | examples | pullback |
|---|---|---|---|
| weight-0 | σ-only | h, w±, labels, knot values | g(σ) = G(s) |
| weight-0 | joint (σ, μ) | Cartesian S1 displacement u | u(σ, μ) = U(s, ν_ε) |
| weight-1 (ruling-linear) | σ-only | μ̂±, clip curves | \|χ′\|·g = Ĝ(s) |
| weight-d (tangent-basis coeff., basis n′-homogeneous deg d) | joint | director (p₁, p₂): d = 1 | \|χ′\|^d·pᵢ(σ, μ) = Pᵢ(s, ν_ε) |
| jets to order k | joint | EDGE rows, import residual jets | corrected triangle: ∂_σ\|_μ = \|χ′\|∂_s + (\|χ′\|)′μ·∂_ν; ∂_μ = \|χ′\|∂_ν; second order adds mixed and tail terms \|χ′\|²∂_ss + 2\|χ′\|(\|χ′\|)′μ·∂_sν + ((\|χ′\|)′μ)²∂_νν + (\|χ′\|)′∂_s + (\|χ′\|)″μ·∂_ν — coefficients polynomial in (χ′, χ″, μ), in-ring, class A; sharing-based attachment transports automatically |
| point data | — | anchors, crease curves, interface endpoints | (σ̂, μ̂) ↦ (s∘σ̂, \|χ′∘σ̂\|·μ̂) — the base map itself |

*Composition in the first argument alone samples the quotient field at the wrong ruling coordinate: a band displacement passes that test while M, T, EDGE, SLAB-S1, and E^phys all silently move.*

Interface ranges stored as ℓ-intervals are invariant outright. **Acceptance criterion (normative):** substitution shall commute with every physical query **at corresponding material points (s, ν_ε)** — worked invariances: ϑ_T (|v| agreeing at corresponding points), the TILT bound ((P₁²+P₂²)|N̄_s|² = (p₁²+p₂²)|n′|²), signed ℓ. **Worked pair:** μ⁺ ≡ 1 *fails* (|χ′|·1 = 2√χ on χ = σ² — the unweighted test admitted this ring violation); μ⁺ = σ·m(σ²) factors per branch (±2χ·m(χ)). **REMOVABLE record: {k, m, χ, per-field typed ledger}**; geometry-only witnesses prove *limit regularity* only (they feed the stall-limit condition, never a substitution).

**Witness certificates.** **SUBST-MONO** (A · total, Sturm): χ′ single-signed per open span — and it *emits the orientation packet* consumed by all transport: {ε; oriented quotient range [χ(σ₀), χ(σ₁)]; ruling-basis orientation bit (R_ε); bound-permutation flag; interface-range orientation}. One producer; consumers never re-derive the negative branch. ε here **is** the stall-end ε below — one per-span sign datum, one owner. **SUBST-REG** (A): |N_χ|² ≥ m on the χ-range (the substituted chart passes its own REG-Q).

**Non-factoring interior stalls:** split at σ* — G1 iff handle equalities hold; CREASE iff ruling lines coincide; planar-hub wedge bridge (a facet in the shared tangent plane between the two boundary rulings); else reject. Post-split spans over factored geometry record the shared χ-primitive as a **derived tag** (shared geometric identity as certificate, not merge); interface ownership rides the spans untouched.

#### 3.2.2 Stall-end spans — hatted calculus

Post-split spans may carry a `stall-end` flag with p = (σ − σ*)^k, **ε = sgn p recorded per span**, p̂ = εp > 0, μ̂ = p̂μ, r̂ = εr̃, n̂′ = εñ′ (ñ′ = n′/p, h̃′ = h′/p — the removability order condition m ≥ k is exact divisibility; the pedal c is ε-invariant). **Semantics — three objects, not one.** The **raw (σ, μ) presentation** is singular at σ* (that is what a stall *is*). The **stored hatted chart** (σ, μ̂, w) ↦ C is **regular at the endpoint whenever the stall-limit condition holds** — full rank, Ĵ > 0, every hatted constituent (c, r̂, n, ψ′, γ′, D) regular through σ* — so regularity-at-the-stall is a *certified* property, not an aspiration. The **surface** along the stall ruling is a **regular planar (flat) point — a flat generator, approached through parabolic developable points** (the shape operator has rank 0 there: n_μ̂ ≡ 0 as on every developable, and n_σ = p̂n̂′ → 0, so κ₁ = κ₂ = 0; "ordinary parabolic" would promise a surviving curved principal direction that does not exist — principal-frame consumers shall read the hatted frame (r̂, n̂′/ρ), never eigenvectors of the vanishing II): **κ₁ = p̂|n̂′|²/Ĵ extends continuously to exactly zero**, while the size field 1/κ₁ = Ĵ/(p̂|n̂′|²) is extended-real with a declared +∞ endpoint value — "every derived field extends" was a false universal with a counterexample the query API itself exported. The mesh size field is **min(s_max, 1/κ₁) normatively**, unifying planar charts (1/κ₁ ≡ ∞) and stall-ends under one cap. At an odd-k split the joint is administrative: each side's hatted chart is regular at the shared endpoint, the interface carrying only the ε flip in span data.

- **Stored domain fields are the hatted bounds** μ̂± = p̂·μ± (in-ring; the *raw* bounds generically pole at σ* — physical extent persisting through the stall forces μ± ~ C/p̂ — and are unstorable). REG-DOM orders μ̂⁻ < μ̂⁺; anchors carry (σ̂, μ̂); interfaces store ℓ-intervals (invariant).
- **Jacobian of the stored map:** det D_{(σ,μ̂,w)}C = (c′ + μ̂r̂′)·n̂′ + w·p̂|n̂′|² ( = det D_{(σ,μ,w)}C / p̂ — one *positive* factor: r = p̂r̂, n′ = p̂n̂′, ∂μ/∂μ̂ = 1/p̂; the earlier "/p" was the tilde-era relation carried into hatted coordinates, orientation-reversing on ε = −1 branches and inconsistent with the κ₁ formula that silently encodes the correct law). SLAB certifies this hatted determinant > 0; since the w-coefficient vanishes at σ*, the corner reduction gains the **stall-limit condition**: (c′ + μ̂r̂′)·n̂′|_{σ*} > 0 at both μ̂-endpoints (two ring evaluations; zero ⇒ secretly factoring → substitution, or degenerate → trim/reject).
- **ℓ = μ̂ρ (ρ = |n̂′|) is signed** — the hat buys cross-split orientation consistency, not nonnegativity; |ℓ| is the distance; bounds are two-sided. At an odd-split joint the spans carry opposite ε, hence opposite r̂ along the same line — absorbed free by interfaces (handles store the unoriented pedal + line).
- Solver contract near stall-ends, **per query class** ("Newton is legal" was the mirrored false universal of the caveat it retracted — regularity is per-map, solver legality is per-query): (1) the **full spatial inverse** (σ, μ̂, w) ← p may attempt interval-Newton/Krawczyk with the roles pinned: **the operator inclusion itself is the certificate** — K(X) ⊂ int X ⇒ existence and uniqueness in X with the iterate as the enclosure (Krawczyk preferred; the N(X) ⊆ X variant admitted with its existence/uniqueness fine print stated), N(X) ∩ X = ∅ the refutation branch, otherwise undecided ⇒ split — three-valued like everything else in §8; **determinant separation det ⊞J ≥ m is only the attempt filter** — a box determinant proves neither contraction nor existence nor conditioning (det = ρ·(good) can hide a near-singular column exactly where the stall makes columns anisotropic: a volume quoted as a length); the optional conditioning annotation is **σ_min(⊞J)² > m via strict Sylvester** — all three leading principal minors of JᵀJ − m·I strictly positive over the box (cleared forms, Bernstein, margin-conditional; the boundary case is zero-margin, correctly unresolved). Nonnegative *leading* minors do **not** give ⪰ 0 — diag(1, 0, −1) has leading minors {1, 0, 0} and an eigenvalue at −1 — and the semidefinite route needs all 2ⁿ−1 principal minors: noted, not taken. The k-th minor is degree 2k in J's entries (degree 6 at k = 3), hence annotation-grade, never a validity input; bracketing/subdivision the unconditional fallback, class-T stamps engine-agnostic and naming the engine; (2) **reduced queries** supply their own derivative separation or stay with root isolation — the Gauss/normal-direction inverse is *structurally singular* at the stall (n_σ = p̂n̂′ → 0 is the stall's definition; near σ* the honest answer is the k-fold degenerate direction through ñ′, flagged as such), and level/azimuth scalars keep Sturm unless separated; (3) **raw-μ queries** remain singular with the existing fallback. MONO-solve is Sturm root isolation, as always — untouched by the caveat and by its retraction alike.
- **Deflation rule by certificate class:** inequality-class predicates on stall-end spans consume hatted fields (a known p-factor annihilates sign conditions at the end); identity-class predicates are deflation-transparent (SHEAR: det = p²·det̃, the constancy identity clears p⁴; the σ³-parametrized cone still evaluates κ_g = tan β through the stall).

### 3.3 Planar
Record: 2D domain, w-range, datum frame **(O, d) with d exactly unit over K** — stored (d_raw, s), s² = d_raw·d_raw adjoined to the manifest (one L1 generator per datum); axes d_raw/s and n×d_raw/s; the edit-closed linear condition d_raw·n = 0 lives on the raw vector. Planar lifts exact over K; freezes on hubs lossless. R(q) is a constructor's convenience; (O, d) persists — datum immunity structural. Development = the frame. Hub-eligible (§5.2). Calibration: O ↦ O + d_shape·n (crease-free panels).

### 3.4 Domain
σ-span × μ̂-interval; bounds constant or positive-weight rational splines of σ. Free boundary covers material with rational conservative margin; interface boundary is exact structure. Content holes impose nothing while one chart's forming spans them (§5.4). **Clipped domains (a new species):** a chart end participating in a closure carries **D^closure = D ∩ {G_i ≥ 0}** — the retained-side field, uniform sign, no per-flank glyph (H_i survives as a raw diagnostic only, never in predicates or gates) — coupled, non-product. Structure on the v1 population: at each σ the fiber is (rectangle) ∩ (half-plane), a convex polygon whose combinatorial type is fixed by the **signs of G_i (retained-side fields) at the four rectangle corners**; corner-sign-change loci are Sturm-isolated σ-events delivering, in one sweep, emptiness detection, stack-face tangency (isolated events emitted as vertices; degenerate intervals ⇒ margin-conditional refinement), the **exact cut-face σ-range** (consumed by MITER-FIT), and free-boundary/stackup-transition crossings as exact vertices. Per-fiber convexity ⇒ fibers connected always; a disconnected σ-support splits into administrative sub-charts over the shared primitive. Certified by **CLIP-DOM** (§8.5); one half-space clip per chart end in v1 (multiple coupled clips = corner joints = the fold-vertex configuration, which forces bands).

### 3.5 Strata and the director
S0 default. S1: **C̃ = M + wT, M = X + u**, u a rational displacement spline; director in **tangential two-parameter form**

```
T = [(1 − |v|²) n + 2v]/(1 + |v|²),   v·n = 0,   tan(ϑ_T/2) = |v|  exactly,
```

v in per-kind tangent bases — strips v = p₁n′ + p₂(n×n′) with polynomial (p₁, p₂) supported on the band; planar v = p₁d + p₂(n×d) from the datum frame. Two parameters, two DOF: no gauge fiber. T·T ≡ 1 is an identity (w exactly along-fiber arclength; persisted thickness physical); denominator 1 + |v|² structural-by-positivity (atom-ledger category, certificate-free). w-lines straight; det DC̃ degree ≤ 2 in w; S0 = (v ≡ 0). **Layer parallelism is S0-only**; on S1 the exact set is straight fibers, exact along-fiber separation, fiber-wise correspondence (tangent-plane variation certified O(w‖DT‖)) — seams are pinned S0 and bands are small, so the strong guarantee holds where copper and thin-layer meshing live. **BAND** = S1 chart in the fold-band role: (u, v) compactly supported, EDGE attachment (§7). Transverse stretch excluded in v1 (the 33-metric block ≡ 1 structurally); fitted-import scalar s_w reserved [D21].

## 4. Flat structure

### 4.1 Development — explicit record

```
D(σ, μ̂) = γ(σ) + μ̂·ρ(σ)·e(ψ(σ)),        ρ = |n̂′|,
```

### 4.6 Anchors, marks, freezes, and the certified classes

**Correspondence objects.** One class, two directions: **anchors** lift authored flat content to chart coordinates; **marks** project chart-authored structure to flat. Both are certified rational splines with uniform stamps.

**Anchors are function objects.** Point item: rational (σ*, μ̂*) inside the certified D⁻¹ enclosure. Regions: anchored boundary loops (shared corners ⇒ exactly closed); interiors via F's certified evaluation; explicit interior covers optional. A lifted mesh is a discrete interior anchor cover. *Scope:* backward-error correspondence is the contract for **content queries**; it is categorically wrong for **solid boundaries**, which consume incidence (shared edges, exact preimages), not distance — proximity is never attachment.

**Exact-over-anchor.** Downstream of â, geometry is exact along the whole curve: the 3D edge is C(â(t), w) — the exact image of the exact flat curve D(â) within ε of the authored one; sidewalls exactly ruled with exactly straight fibers of exact along-fiber length; both trace faces exact images of one perturbed curve (zero top/bottom registration); prisms extrude exactly; γ_shear w-independent along fibers. **Two certified tiers.** *Algebraic-certified:* inversion of a **3D point** to chart coordinates (rational critical equations; Sturm-decidable predicates; pole-free bracketing by REG-RAT). *Transcendental-certified:* anything through D — **including flat-vertex lifting** (D⁻¹ of a flat point is class T; boundary vertices ride stored anchor splines; interior vertices amortize by marching), F, J, placements, marks, traces — refinable stamps.

**Freezes (the sole certified→authored gate).** Three species:
1. **Frozen curve** — snap a certified mark/projection to exact flat content; provenance {source construction, atlas revision, projection stamp ε, declared intent tolerance τ}; tube containment (source enclosure tube ⊆ the frozen curve's ε-tube, sup-norm placement term included).
2. **Frozen scalar dimension** — {L̂, ε_L, τ}; **containment relation, normative**: I_L ⊆ [L̂ − ε_L, L̂ + ε_L] with I_L the certified source enclosure after full propagation; operationally **ε_L ≥ radius(I_L) + |L̂ − mid(I_L)|** (both terms mandatory). Two refinement verbs: *tighten parents* (radius) and *re-snap L̂* (a record mutation with provenance; placement).
3. **Frozen scalar field** — L̂(t) a rational spline with **uniform** containment: I_L(t) ⊆ [L̂(t) ± ε_L] for all t (per-span stamps legal refinement structure).

**Provenance chain rule:** every freeze stamp is computed from, and bounded below by, the certified enclosures of its source evaluations; no freeze from a naked float. **FRESH is a containment re-test**, three-way: regenerated enclosure ⊆ stamp interval → green; **disjoint → stale, definitively** — a refutation that hard-fails the export gate for promoted values; partial overlap → undecided(margin), refine or re-freeze. τ is the intent layer on top ("has the world moved more than I care"), never absorbing arithmetic falsity. **Promotion keys on role:** only fab-gating fields (materialStripWidth, fab-critical frozen curves) are FRESH-promoted by default.
